Instructor, Langara College Film Arts, and Artistic Associate for Mitch &amp; Murray Productions. Thank you to Aaron and Kate for your constant support and belief in me!

## Rhonda Dent — Producer

Rhonda Dent is a five-time Leo-nominated Canadian actress, writer, producer, and director with over 25 years of professional experience. She is a graduate of the University College of the Fraser Valley’s theatre program and Langara College’s Film Arts Directing program. Rhonda has appeared in numerous film and television productions, including leading roles in Lifetime Movies of the Week. A lifelong storyteller, Rhonda has produced over ten live theatre productions through Paige 18 Productions, while writing and producing original work for the screen. She is currently developing Footprints, a feature-length Sasquatch adventure mystery set in the ancient forests of the Pacific Northwest, slated to film in Fall 2027.

## Ben Elliott — Sound Designer

Ben Elliott is a Vancouver-based sound designer, composer and musical director whose work spans two decades on stages across Canada and the United States. Credits include Comedy of Errors (Bard on the Beach), I, Claudia, King Charles III and Other Desert Cities (Arts Club), Our Man in Havana (Vertigo/Persephone) and A Christmas Carol (Pacific Theatre). He has composed original scores for Cymbeline and Measure for Measure at Bard, King Lear and Hamlet for the Honest Fishmonger’s Co-op, and the TV pilots Hello Charlie and Humantown. He won a Sterling Award for his musical direction of A Midsummer Night’s Dream: The ’70s Musical (The Citadel) and a Jessie Award for musical direction (The Merry Wives of Windsor), and has received a Dora Mavor Moore Award nomination (RETOLD, The Musical Stage Company) and Jessie nominations for sound design and original score. Ben is a graduate of Studio 58.

## Elizabeth McCarthy Meek — Publicity &amp; Social Media
