Mark Rothko
02
Soft-edged rectangles of colour floating on vast canvases, meant to be seen up close.
1903Dvinsk — New York1970

Born Marcus Rothkowitz in Dvinsk, in the Russian Empire (today Daugavpils, Latvia), Rothko emigrated with his family to Portland, Oregon, at the age of ten. After two years at Yale he left for New York, studied at the Art Students League and began painting city scenes and figures.

Through the 1940s his work moved from myth and surrealism toward pure abstraction. He became a central figure of Abstract Expressionism, though he rejected the label and insisted his paintings were about human emotion.

In 1958 he accepted a commission to paint murals for the Four Seasons restaurant in New York’s Seagram Building. He later withdrew and returned the fee.

The Play
A fierce argument about art, commerce, mortality, and the generation that will one day take the master’s place. Draft · please review

John Logan’s Red premiered at London’s Donmar Warehouse in December 2009 and transferred to Broadway the following year, where it won six Tony Awards, including Best Play.

Logan, screenwriter of Gladiator, Hugo and Skyfall, imagines Rothko at work on the Seagram murals with Ken, a young assistant of the playwright’s invention. Over two years in the studio the two men circle each other through argument, labour and silence.

Producer
Rhonda
Dent
Rhonda Dent is a five-time Leo-nominated Canadian actress, writer, producer, and director with over 25 years of professional experience. She is a graduate of the University College of the Fraser Valley’s theatre program and Langara College’s Film Arts Directing program. Rhonda has appeared in numerous film and television productions, including leading roles in Lifetime Movies of the Week. A lifelong storyteller, Rhonda has produced over ten live theatre productions through Paige 18 Productions, while writing and producing original work for the screen. She is currently developing Footprints, a feature-length Sasquatch adventure mystery set in the ancient forests of the Pacific Northwest, slated to film in Fall 2027.

Starlynn Chen (she/her) is an emerging set and costume designer and a graduate of the Production Program at Studio 58. Recent credits: set and costume design for The Birds (Studio 58), Avenue Q (Metro Theatre); set design for Running Wilde (United Players), The Paper Bag Princess (Carousel Theatre), A Journal of the Plague Year (Studio 58), Jack and the Beanstalk (Metro Theatre), and Linck and Mülhahn (Studio 58); costume design for A Streetcar Named Desire & Peerless (ETC), The Undeniable Accusations of Red Cadmium Light (Firehall Arts Centre), The Replacement Wife (United Players), The Odd Couple (Theatre NorthWest), and James and the Giant Peach (CTORA). Lighting Designer
Sam Cheng
Originally from Hong Kong, Sam is an emerging artist from UBC Theatre Design and Production, specializing in scenic and lighting design and technical direction. Select lighting design credits: The Lawyer Show: 9 to 5 (Touchstone Theatre), A Streetcar Named Desire (Ensemble Theatre Company), Citysong (VitalSpark Theatre), Pippin (Play On Words Production), Salt-Water Moon (Western Gold Theatre), and Metamorphoses (United Players of Vancouver). samchengwx.com
Sound Designer
Ben Elliott
Ben Elliott is a Vancouver-based sound designer, composer and musical director whose work spans two decades on stages across Canada and the United States. Credits include Comedy of Errors (Bard on the Beach), I, Claudia, King Charles III and Other Desert Cities (Arts Club), Our Man in Havana (Vertigo/Persephone) and A Christmas Carol (Pacific Theatre). He has composed original scores for Cymbeline and Measure for Measure at Bard, King Lear and Hamlet for the Honest Fishmonger’s Co-op, and the TV pilots Hello Charlie and Humantown. He won a Sterling Award for his musical direction of A Midsummer Night’s Dream: The ’70s Musical (The Citadel) and a Jessie Award for musical direction (The Merry Wives of Windsor), and has received a Dora Mavor Moore Award nomination (RETOLD, The Musical Stage Company) and Jessie nominations for sound design and original score. Ben is a graduate of Studio 58.
